My tierlist for the 2026 Kelowna counciller election.

I spent about 7 hours going through the list of candidates looking at all their platforms and briefly at their backgrounds. They all probably deserve more time looking into but there's 37 candidates. **Do not treat this as some kind of be-all end all tier list**, it's just a collection of my findings that you may or may not find useful.
The order within each tier is just the order I came across them.

**I'm mainly looking at their platforms since that shows where their values are**, and some issues don't have a one-size-fits-all solution. For example, you can't have affordable housing without lowering housing costs, which results in homeowners losing some potential money if they were wanting to sell, and some wealthy homeowners will be *very* vocal about this. Also some think that traffic can be fixed by demolishing businesses and residences to expand roads ("just one more lane!") but cars don't scale, and thus we *need* better public transportation for traffic to ever improve.

Though backgrounds are also important for knowing where they're experienced and if they have any conflicts of interest.

**If you have something you wanted to add about a particular candidate (particularly their values), feel free to do so!** I don't have any particularly strong feelings about my placements (other than those BRACE populist arsehats, their platforms are AI abominations) and I absolutely could've missed some things. Just feel free to DM me or leave a comment on whatever platform you found this list.

My values for a candidate are:

- Reducing car dependency (via public transportation, better zoning, etc.)
- Housing affordability
- Transparency and honesty

Also is ranking a bunch of people with complex beliefs systems incredibly reductive? Yes. But with most media outlets and platforms being owned by conservative media conglomerates or anger-maximizing engagement social media algorithms (and more importantly the fact that there's 37 candidates), meh.

<h2 class="mt-10 border-b border-[var(--line)] pb-2 text-2xl font-semibold tracking-tight text-[var(--ink)]">Observations</h2>

- Most candidates usually follow the formula of: "Kelowna has XYZ problems, we need to do [vague, high-level goal]" but very few actually have *specific ideas* on how these goals will be accomplished. 
	- For example, most platforms mention that we should "cut red tape" for fixing housing affordability, but none of them mention specific policies that should be changed. And then in the *same breath* they sometimes mention "we need to make sure the infrastructure supports the new building and make sure it fits in with existing neighborhoods" i.e. *more red tape* (though note that I'm not necessarily against these ideas in principal, it's just kinda contradictory) 
- A vast majority of candidates utilized AI in some regard. In campaign posters, platforms, about pages, and more. Some used it more than others. Like some probably wrote their platform beforehand and then just used AI to build the website after the fact. Others used AI heavily in the constructing of their whole platform (see BRACE candidates), and you can usually tell by how overconfident the platform is (while also lacking specifics).
