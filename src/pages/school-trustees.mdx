There's only 6 candidates, 4 to be elected. None seem to be joke candidates, so that's good! I'd recommend checking out [kelownaelections.ca](https://kelownaelections.ca/candidates/joyce-brinkerhoff/) and this [castanet article](https://www.castanet.net/news/Kelowna/633478/Meet-the-Kelowna-school-trusteee-candidates) since their individual websites don't have much to go by.

All of their platforms are mostly just vague "more betterer", with not much difference between their platforms.

I also don't see any big red flag backgrounds. Like they all seem pretty qualified and they're not super radical on social media.

So like they're all like B-tier or C-tier.

One difference is their view on [SOGI](https://teachers.ab.ca/news/what-sogi-123) (it's a list of optional resources for teachers to help inclusivity in their classrooms, e.g. saying "good morning class/everybody" instead of "good morning boys and girls". It's rather minute but anyone who views LGBTQ people as their out-group hates it). You could use their view on SOGI as an extension of their wider belief system (or at least what their social media algorithm is convincing them of).

Pro SOGI:

- Richard Gauthier
- Val Johnson

Anti SOGI:

- Deb Dickerson-Pogue

No Mention of SOGI:

- Joyce Brinkerhoff
- Jim Strachan
- Ashok Kumar Verma

Verdict: we're probably not gonna be screwed if you choose not to vote for any school trustees.
